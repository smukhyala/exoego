Where Exo Wins

An exocentric camera is better than a head-mounted one for reading what task is happening — but only if it is close enough to see hands. Move it to the ceiling and the advantage reverses. Exo's value is a property of framing, not of being exocentric.

What this figure does and does not claim. It claims that exo's advantage at task reading depends on camera framing, and that a ceiling camera over a whole bay loses that advantage because it cannot resolve hands. It does not claim a worker-coverage multiplier — that could not be measured reliably with an off-the-shelf detector on overhead views.

Assembly101 figures: segment-level linear probe, 8 seeds, N=143 labelled sequences. World Context figures: 179 synchronized windows from a paired GoPro ego/exo recording, aligned at +11.16 s by onset cross-correlation. Both World Context cameras are the same model at 1920×1080 colour, so resolution and colour are controlled and what differs is viewpoint.
